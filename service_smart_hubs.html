Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

Can I control everything from my iPhone and Apple Watch?

Yes! With Apple HomeKit and Matter integration, you can control lights, locks, and climate directly from Control Center on your iPhone or Apple Watch.

What happens if my internet goes down?

Matter and Thread devices communicate locally over your home's wireless mesh network, so switches and schedules continue working even during internet outages.
