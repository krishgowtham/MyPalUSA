The 4-Step Precision Installation

Our systematic, white-glove approach guarantees exact execution from initial measurement to clean-up.

1. Structural Joist & Box Verification

We inspect your ceiling electrical box to ensure it is fan-rated. If it is a standard plastic light box, we install a heavy-duty expanding steel joist brace.

2. Motor Assembly & Safety Cable

We thread internal wiring through the downrod, lock safety hitch pins, and anchor the safety cable directly into structural framing.

3. Electrical Wiring & RF Receiver

Connections are made with high-temperature wire nuts, ground wires bonded to the junction box, and the wireless receiver module neatly tucked into the canopy.

4. Dynamic High-Speed Balancing

Blades are attached and tracked at maximum speed. We apply counterweight clips to eliminate 100% of wobbling and noise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

Can you install a ceiling fan where only a small light fixture used to be?

Yes! Standard light fixtures use lightweight plastic boxes not rated for vibrations. We install an expanding steel joist brace between ceiling joists to safely hold heavy fans.

Why do fans wobble, and can you fix mine?

Wobble is caused by weight or aerodynamic pitch discrepancies between blades. We track blades with laser guides and attach precision balancing weights for whisper-quiet rotation.

Can I control the fan and light separately?

Yes! If you have a 3-wire electrical run, we wire independent wall switches. We can also install modern RF or smart controllers that operate both functions over standard wiring.
