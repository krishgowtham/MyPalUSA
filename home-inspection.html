COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about scheduling, preparation, and reports.

What is a Pre-Drywall Inspection and when should it be scheduled? ▼
A Pre-Drywall (also known as Rough-In or Framing) Inspection occurs during new home construction after framing, electrical wiring, plumbing supply/DWV pipes, and HVAC ductwork are installed, but immediately before insulation and drywall are hung. This is your single opportunity to inspect the structural bones, load-bearing headers, hurricane strapping, and mechanical routing while everything is fully exposed.
How does a Buyer's Pre-Purchase Inspection differ from a Pre-Drywall Inspection? ▼
A Buyer's Pre-Purchase Inspection evaluates a completed home (either existing resale or newly finished construction) during your contract due diligence window. It focuses on whole-home operational systems, appliances, roof, crawlspace, plumbing, and cosmetic/mechanical defects. A Pre-Drywall Inspection evaluates the raw framing, rough MEP (mechanical, electrical, plumbing) systems, and structural integrity before sheetrock closes off access.
Why should I get an 11-Month Builder Warranty Inspection? ▼
Most new home builders provide a 1-year comprehensive warranty. Over the first year, homes settle, resulting in drywall cracks, door sticking, attic duct disconnections, and grading shifts. An 11-Month Warranty Inspection gives you an unbiased, professional punch-list with high-resolution photos that you submit directly to your builder before their 12-month obligation expires.
How long does a full home inspection take? ▼
A standard single-family residential inspection takes between 3 and 4 hours depending on the home's square footage, foundation type (slab vs. crawlspace), and age. We take the time necessary to thoroughly examine all accessible spaces.
Should I attend the inspection in person? ▼
We strongly recommend attending the final 45 to 60 minutes for the on-site walk-through. Our inspector will explain all findings in plain English, show you emergency water and electrical shut-offs, and answer any questions before the report is finalized.
How quickly will I receive my digital inspection report? ▼
We deliver your complete, high-resolution digital PDF report within 24 hours of inspection completion (often same-day). Every report includes high-resolution color photos, categorized defect severity, and an executive repair punch-list formatted specifically for real estate contract negotiations.
What geographic areas in North Carolina do you cover? ▼
We service the entire North Carolina Research Triangle and surrounding municipalities: Raleigh, Cary, Apex, Morrisville, Wake Forest, Durham, Chapel Hill, Holly Springs, Fuquay-Varina, Garner, and Clayton.
