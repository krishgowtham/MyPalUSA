Started in 2023

MyPalUSA was established in 2023 in North Carolina to solve a growing problem: homeowners were forced to choose between overpriced commercial contractors and unreliable handymen. We set out to bridge that gap with certified expertise and everyday friendliness.

Great Value for Less Money

Our core mission is straightforward: premium workmanship should never cost a fortune. By optimizing our tooling, streamlining dispatch, and cutting corporate overhead, we deliver white-glove installations at prices significantly lower than big-box retail competitors.

Good Intentions, Honest Service

We approach every project with genuine integrity. We treat your home as if it were our own — ensuring zero drywall damage, laser-level alignments, concealed wiring, and complete cleanup. Our goal is simple: helping everyone enjoy a safer, smarter, and more beautiful home.
