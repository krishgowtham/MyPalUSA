The 4-Step Precision Installation

Our systematic, white-glove approach guarantees exact execution from initial measurement to clean-up.

1. Joist Center Detection

We locate joist centers using deep-penetration multi-sensor scanners to guarantee solid bite.

2. Pilot Drilling & Lag Bolting

Pilot holes prevent wood splitting, followed by 3" industrial lag bolts torqued to spec.

3. Vertical Drop Calibration

Angle posts are dropped at custom heights clearing garage door tracks with 3-5" buffer.

4. Wire Grid & Load Test

Steel wire decking is fastened with lock rivets and a 600-lb structural load check conducted.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

What is the maximum garage ceiling height for overhead racks?

We install overhead racks on residential garage ceilings up to 10 to 12 feet maximum. Ultra-high commercial ceilings exceeding 12 feet are not supported.

Can you install in finished drywall ceilings?

Yes! Over 90% of our installations are in finished drywall garages. We locate joist centers with precision digital scanners.

Will it interfere with my garage door?

We measure maximum open door height and adjust drop posts to ensure safe clearance above moving tracks.
