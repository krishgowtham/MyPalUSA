The 4-Step Precision Installation

Our systematic, white-glove approach guarantees exact execution from initial measurement to clean-up.

1. Stud Mapping & Viewing Angle Calculation

We scan for studs, plumbing, and live wiring, calculating optimal eye-level viewing height from your seating positions.

2. Heavy-Duty Structural Lag Bolting

The steel bracket is laser-leveled and secured directly into structural framing with heavy-duty lag bolts.

3. In-Wall Power Bridge Installation

We install an ETL-listed in-wall power bridge to safely hide power cords and HDMI cables behind the drywall.

4. Display Mounting & Fine-Tuning

The TV is locked onto the bracket, safety latches clicked, tilt tension adjusted, and streaming inputs verified.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

Can you hide all cables inside the wall legally?

Yes! We install ETL-listed, in-wall high-voltage power relocation bridges that meet National Electrical Code (NEC) standards, so no extension cords are hidden behind drywall.

Can you mount a TV above a fireplace or into brick?

Yes! We specialize in masonry, brick, and tile fireplace installations using specialized diamond-carbide bits and heavy-duty masonry anchors.

Do I need to supply the bracket?

You can supply your own bracket or we can provide commercial-grade fixed, tilting, or articulating brackets tested for your exact display.
