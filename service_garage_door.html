The 4-Step Precision Installation

Our systematic, white-glove approach guarantees exact execution from initial measurement to clean-up.

1. Logic Board Testing

We test Wi-Fi signal at the ceiling and wire smart dry-contact relays into opener terminals.

2. Hub & Tilt Sensor Mounting

Controller is anchored to the ceiling and wireless tilt sensor fixed to top door panel.

3. Safety Beam Alignment

Optical infrared safety reversing beams are calibrated and obstruction tested.

4. App Setup & Alerts

We set up push notifications for doors left open over 10 minutes and test arrival geofencing.
