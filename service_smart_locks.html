The 4-Step Precision Installation

Our systematic, white-glove approach guarantees exact execution from initial measurement to clean-up.

1. Door & Jamb Inspection

We check door alignment to ensure zero friction when the deadbolt extends.

2. Chisel & Strike Tuning

We deepen the jamb mortise so the motorized deadbolt slides smoothly with zero resistance.

3. Lock Assembly & Sealing

Exterior keypad and interior motor drive are installed with weatherproof seals.

4. App Provisioning & PINs

We configure the mobile app, set up family PINs, and enable auto-lock.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

What happens if the batteries die?

All smart deadbolts include exterior 9V emergency jump terminals or physical backup key cylinders so you will never be locked out.

Why do batteries drain fast on some doors?

Misaligned deadbolts force the motor to work hard against the strike plate. We chisel and align the jamb so it glides with zero friction.
