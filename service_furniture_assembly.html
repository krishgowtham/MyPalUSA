The 4-Step Precision Installation

Our systematic, white-glove approach guarantees exact execution from initial measurement to clean-up.

1. Staging & Inventory

We lay down clean blankets, unbox parts, and verify hardware against parts manifests.

2. Structural Assembly

We assemble frame components with torque-controlled drivers and joint reinforcement.

3. Alignment & Squaring

Cabinet frames are squared, drawer slides leveled, and soft-close hinges tuned.

4. Anti-Tip Stud Anchoring

Tall units are anchored directly into wooden wall studs with heavy-duty steel safety straps.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

Do you anchor tall furniture to the wall?

Yes! Every tall dresser, bookcase, and wardrobe includes child-safety anti-tip anchoring into solid wall studs in compliance with the STURDY Act.

What if parts or hardware are missing?

Our technicians carry spare metric bolts, cam locks, wooden dowels, and pins so your project is never delayed by a missing factory screw.
