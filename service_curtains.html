The 4-Step Precision Installation

Our systematic, white-glove approach guarantees exact execution from initial measurement to clean-up.

1. Elevation & Width Layout

We calculate optimal rod height (4-8 inches above casing) and stack-back extension.

2. Laser Alignment & Anchoring

Brackets are laser-leveled and secured into studs or heavy-duty toggle anchors.

3. Center Support & Saddle Lock

Center brackets are installed to prevent sag, and the rod locked into saddles.

4. Drapery Hanging & Pleating

Panels are hung with even ring spacing, pleats trained, and finials hand-tightened.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

What ceiling height is supported for curtain rod installations?

We mount drapery rods and ceiling tracks on residential walls up to 10 to 12 feet maximum with standard ladders. Multi-story 2-story foyers or heights exceeding 12 feet requiring commercial scaffolding are not supported.

How high should curtain rods be mounted?

Designers recommend mounting 4 to 8 inches above window trim (or just below crown molding) to make ceilings look taller.

Will heavy velvet curtains pull out of drywall?

Never with our installations. We anchor brackets directly into wall studs and use 1/4" steel toggle bolts rated for 80+ lbs in hollow walls.
