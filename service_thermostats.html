Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before your installation appointment.

Why does my smart thermostat reboot unexpectedly?

This is caused by a missing 24V Common (C) wire. The thermostat attempts to 'power steal', causing resets. We install a C-wire kit to permanently fix this.

Will it work with heat pump emergency heat?

Yes! We configure the W2/AUX terminal and set the outdoor compressor lock-out temperature so backup heat only triggers when necessary.
